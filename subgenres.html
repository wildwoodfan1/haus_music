House Music Subgenres

Explore the sounds, tempos, and icons shaping house tunes

Tech House

122–126 BPM

The most-booked peak-time club sound, blending a steady 4/4 house groove with the driving, stripped-back percussion and energy of techno.

Key Artists: FISHER, John Summit, Mau P

Deep House

118–124 BPM

A warm, soulful, and moody style built around rich chords, atmospheric pads, and a slower-to-mid tempo used for after-hours and deep listening.

Progressive House

124–128 BPM

A festival-leaning and hypnotic style featuring longer builds, layered melodies, and gradual tension-building rather than instant drops.

Afro House

120–125 BPM

A rapidly surging, global sound driven by organic African percussive rhythms, deep sub-basses, and chant vocals.

Classic / Chicago House

120–127 BPM

The foundation of all house music, birthed in 1980s Chicago clubs like the Warehouse. Characterized by raw Roland TR-909 drum patterns, soulful disco samples, synth basslines, and uplifting gospel-inspired vocals.

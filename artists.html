Black Coffee

Black Coffee is a South African DJ and producer known for his deep house music and unique African influences. His music combines smooth rhythms and soulful sounds that have made him popular around the world.

Avicii

Avicii was a Swedish DJ and producer who helped bring electronic dance music into mainstream music. He was known for combining EDM with pop and songs like "Wake Me Up" and "Levels."

Fisher

Fisher is an Australian DJ and producer known for his energetic house music and entertaining performances. His song "Losing It" became a worldwide hit and helped establish him as one of the biggest names in house music.

Frankie Knuckles

Frankie Knuckles was an American DJ and producer widely known as the "Godfather of House Music." His work in Chicago helped shape house music into a genre that influenced dance music around the world.

Disclosure

Disclosure is an English electronic music duo made up of brothers Guy and Howard Lawrence. They are known for blending house music with UK garage, R&B, and pop, creating songs like "Latch."
